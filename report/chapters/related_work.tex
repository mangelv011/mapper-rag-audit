\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Mapper and TDA on text.}
Mapper builds a graph from a lens function, an overlapping cover of its
range and a clustering of each preimage~\cite{singh2007topological}.
Rathore et al.~\cite{rathore2023topobert} apply Mapper to the embeddings of
fine-tuned BERT models to see how fine-tuning changes them.
KeplerMapper~\cite{vanveen2019kepler} and
tda-mapper~\cite{simi2025tdamapper} are maintained Python implementations.

\paragraph{Mapper on RAG corpora.}
Prior work applies Mapper to embedding spaces of language
models~\cite{rathore2023topobert}. We compare the corpus before and after
an ingestion; we found no prior work that uses Mapper to choose which RAG
test questions to re-run.

\paragraph{Regression test selection.}
To cut the cost of re-running a test suite, regression testing
\emph{minimises}, \emph{selects} or \emph{prioritises}
tests~\cite{yoo2012regression}. Rothermel and Harrold~\cite{rothermel1997safe}
select tests safely by comparing control-flow graphs of the old and new
program. In our case the data changes, not the code, and the Mapper
graphs before and after the ingestion take the place of the control-flow
graphs.
