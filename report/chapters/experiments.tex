\section{Experiments}

Section~\ref{sec:method} defines the corpus, retrieval, ingestions, selectors
and evaluation measures. We run every selector on the 30 ingestions at the
three budgets with one command:
\begin{quote}
\texttt{python experiments/main\_experiment.py}
\end{quote}
The script saves the raw runs and their summary under \texttt{results/} and
writes the table and figure of Section~\ref{sec:results}.
