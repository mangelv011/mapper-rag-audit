\section{Experiments}

\subsection{SciFact trap ingestions}

We used the SciFact test split~\cite{wadden2020scifact} as distributed by
BEIR~\cite{thakur2021beir}, with 300 questions and 5,658 chunks
from 5,183 abstracts. We embedded the chunks and questions with
\texttt{BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5} and ranked chunks by cosine similarity.
A question passed if at least one chunk from a relevant document appeared
among the five highest-ranked chunks.

We ran three independent ingestions from the same original corpus. Each
added 20 synthetic chunks targeting the same 20 distinct questions that
passed before ingestion, sampled using seed 42. A \texttt{near\_copy}
alters details in a relevant chunk. A \texttt{contradiction} uses
rule-based edits intended to reverse or negate a finding. A \texttt{mix}
combines part of a relevant chunk with part of a chunk from a different
document, sampled from the lowest quartile of cosine similarity to the source
chunk. The original relevance judgments were used in every run.

We checked all 300 questions after each ingestion. A regression is a
question that passed on the original corpus and failed after ingestion. We
report regressions among targeted questions and across all questions that
initially passed.
